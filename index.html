Plan for a different wind (or no signal)

Spots are scored inside legal firearm/bow preserves, at least ~40 yd from boundaries and outside the 100-yd house zones. Scoring favors a morning ground blind on flat ground near deer travel (benches, draws, ridges) between food (oaks, fields) and bedding (swamps, thick conifer, benches), with the wind and early-morning thermals carrying your scent away from them. How to use

  • ◎ follows your GPS location. The bottom card shows whether you're in a legal zone, the distance to the boundary, and shooting hours.
  • Tap the map to check any spot. Long-press or tap ＋ to drop a waypoint (stand, blind, camera, scrape…) with notes and photos.
  • ● records your track. Layers has one-tap presets: Terrain scout (LiDAR relief, contours, benches/ridges/draws), Stand finder (satellite, relief, slope, contours), Food & cover (leaf-off photos, forest types, wetlands).
  • Tapping a spot shows its elevation, slope, which way it faces, terrain feature and forest type, even offline.
  • Morning hunt plan (tap the wind at top-left): pick your hunt day to see the sunrise-window wind, then tap Find my best blind spots for the top 5 places to set a blind, with which way to face and where to walk in from.
  • Scouting tip: look for a bench (green) on a steep side-hill above a draw (blue) or creek, close to oak or pine-oak (red/orange in Forest types). Check your wind before you sit.
  • Share (top bar) gives your buddies the app by QR code or link. Any waypoint can be texted as a link that opens in their app.

Before you hunt

  • This app is a guide, not legal advice. Boundaries come from state GIS data and may be off by tens of feet, and phone GPS can be off by 15–50+ feet. Posted signs and blazes on the ground take priority.
  • Zone status comes from the Freeport Conservation Trust map Hunting on Freeport Conservation Trust Preserves. Confirm current rules with FCT (207-865-3985). Rules can change during a season.
  • Maine: no Sunday hunting. Legal shooting time is 30 min before sunrise to 30 min after sunset (the times shown are calculated). Follow license, tag and orange-clothing rules from Maine IF&W.
  • The 100-yard circles use E911 address points, which can be off from the actual building, and some buildings have no address. Use your eyes.
  • The forest layers are satellite/model estimates (30 m and 250 m), so use them to decide where to look. Confirm oaks on foot.

Data

Boundaries: Maine Conserved Lands. LiDAR elevation and leaf-off orthoimagery: Maine GeoLibrary. Address points: Maine E911. Hydrography and topo: USGS. Forest types: LANDFIRE 2024 EVT (USGS/USFS). Wetlands: USFWS National Wetlands Inventory. Imagery: Esri. Weather: Open-Meteo. Hunting status: Freeport Conservation Trust. Zone data built .
